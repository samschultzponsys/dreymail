Privacy

DreyMail on this server. Last updated November 15, 2026.

DreyMail is self-hosted mail software. This copy of DreyMail is run by the person or organization that operates this server (the “operator”), not by a company that collects your data. Your mail stays with your mail providers; DreyMail shows it to you and sends what you write.

What this server keeps

  • Your DreyMail account: name, username or sign-in identity, profile picture, role and settings.
  • The mailboxes you add: server addresses and the password or sign-in token for each, encrypted (AES-256-GCM) with a key only this server holds. Passwords and tokens are never sent back to your browser.
  • A disposable, encrypted copy of your newest messages (to load quickly), and what you set up in DreyMail itself: signatures, rules, snoozes, scheduled messages, blocked senders, calendars you make in DreyMail, and notification subscriptions.

Google and Microsoft accounts

When you add Gmail with Google sign-in, or Outlook with Microsoft sign-in, DreyMail asks for access to your mail and calendar. It uses that access only to show you your mail and calendar, to send, move and delete messages and change events when you ask it to, and to tell you about new mail. It does not use the data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not share it with anyone else. People can't read it except where you ask for it or the law requires it.

DreyMail’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The AI mailbox assistant (optional)

The assistant is off unless you turn it on for a mailbox. When it is on, DreyMail sends the parts of messages you allow (set with its accuracy slider) to the AI service you connected, only to sort your mail by your rules. With a local model (such as LM Studio or Ollama) nothing leaves your network. That service’s own privacy terms apply to what it receives.

What leaves this server

  • Connections to your mail and calendar providers, to read and send what you ask for.
  • Remote images and contact pictures are fetched by the server only when shown, so senders and picture services don’t see your address.
  • Notifications go through your browser’s or phone’s push service, encrypted end to end.
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising of any kind.

Removing your data

Removing a mailbox in DreyMail deletes its stored password or token and cached mail from this server. You can also take back access at any time in your Google account (Security → Third-party connections) or Microsoft account. The operator can remove your DreyMail account entirely.

Questions

Contact the operator of this server. DreyMail’s source code is public, so you can see exactly what it does.
